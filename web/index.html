Variational Quantum Monte Carlo

Estimate the ground-state energy of the one-dimensional quantum harmonic oscillator using Metropolis sampling of a Gaussian trial wavefunction.

Runs locally · No account or server

Simulation parameters

All calculations happen on this device. Changing the random seed changes the Monte Carlo trajectory.

Physical model

H = −½ d²/dx² + ½ x²

ψα(x) = exp(−αx²/2)

E_local = α/2 + (1−α²)x²/2

The analytic variational expectation is (α + 1/α)/4. The exact ground energy is 0.5 (ℏ = m = ω = 1).
